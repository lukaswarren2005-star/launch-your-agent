# Strategy, hooks, scripts and copy

The thinking before any generation is spent. Cheap to iterate, and it decides whether the content works. Ad-copy frameworks are adapted from Caleb Kruse's `meta-ad-builder` copy guide (MIT, see `../ATTRIBUTION.md`).

## 1. The brief (fill it from the project profile in memory; ask only for gaps)

| Field | One line |
|---|---|
| Brand + product | Which business, which SKU/offer |
| Audience | Who, in one sentence, and what they already believe |
| Job | What the viewer should feel or do after |
| Angle | The one idea (see angle bank) |
| Proof | One concrete fact, number or demonstration |
| Hook | First 1–2 seconds / first line |
| CTA | Exact words |
| Channel + format | Paid Meta, TikTok organic, IG Reel, carousel, YouTube, email… and aspect ratio |
| Constraints | Banned claims/words, length, must-include |

Translate vague adjectives into visuals: "premium" → matte materials, slow camera, black void, restrained type; "fun" → bright daylight, fast cuts, laughing mid-line.

## 2. Angle bank

Pick 3 genuinely different bets per brief, not 3 edits of one idea.

- **Problem you didn't know you had** — reveal a hidden cost ("your shower water touches your skin every day").
- **Before / after (the math)** — old way vs new way, with numbers.
- **Skeptic converted** — "I thought this was a gimmick…"
- **Us vs them** — vs the default category (`competitor_category`).
- **Mechanism** — show *how* it works; the reason to believe.
- **Founder story** — why this exists, first person.
- **Social proof / authority** — counts, reviews, press, credentials (only real ones).
- **Objection handler** — name the doubt (price, install, does it work) and dismantle it.
- **Identity** — "for people who…".
- **Routine / day-in-life** — the product as a natural part of a day.

## 3. Hook bank (first 1–2 s or first line)

- Call-out: "If you {have this problem}, stop scrolling."
- Hidden truth: "Nobody tells you {surprising fact}."
- Mistake: "You're {doing common thing} wrong."
- Result-first: "{Outcome} in {time}. Here's how."
- Confession: "Okay, I was wrong about {product category}."
- Pattern interrupt: an unexpected action in frame (twisting it off the shower, dropping it, a reaction face) before any words.
- Question: "Why does nobody {obvious-in-hindsight thing}?"
- POV / text-on-screen: "POV: you finally {fixed the thing}."

Write 5–10 hooks per angle, then pick the 3 sharpest. A hook must make sense with the sound off.

## 4. Video script shape

`Hook (0–2 s) → Problem or tension → Proof / demo → Payoff → CTA`. For 15 s: 3–4 beats, 26–35 spoken words max, one silent beat. Write it as the person would say it out loud. Read it aloud and time it.

Deliver scripts as a table: beat · seconds · on-screen action · spoken line · on-screen text (if any).

## 5. Paid ad copy (Meta-style: 5 bodies / 5 titles / 3 descriptions)

Meta mixes them per impression, so give it real variety.

**The 5 bodies — one per angle:**
1. **Reveal / story** — first person, present tense, "here's what just happened". Longest.
2. **The math / before-after** — old way as timed steps → total; new way → total.
3. **Fresh hook** — a pattern-breaking line that reframes the category.
4. **Ultra-short CTA** — one claim, 3 arrow bullets, one CTA. Often wins on cold traffic.
5. **Authority / community** — credibility first, then what they get.

Swap-ins: tool/ingredient reveal · mechanism · objection handler.

**Titles (≈40 chars):** outcome · identity · direct CTA. **Descriptions (≈30–50 chars):** a proof stat · what's included · value framing.

**Conventions:** choppy line breaks; `→` bullets; every body ends on the action; specific numbers beat adjectives; one offer, one link. For Reels-style placements use a comment CTA ("Comment FILTER and I'll send the link") matching the spoken CTA.

**Modelling winners:** when top-performing ads are available (Motion creative analytics, Windsor, Meta Ad Library), read *why* each wins — hook pattern, proof, CTA — and carry the pattern, not the words.

## 6. Organic content

**Pillars** — each project keeps 3–5 in its profile, e.g. education (how/why), proof (results, reviews, demos), behind the scenes (founder, making it), entertainment (trends, humour), offer (launches, drops). Aim most posts at education/proof/BTS; offers are the minority.

**Formats by channel**

| Channel | Formats that work | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | Talking head, POV text-on-screen, demo, green-screen reaction, trend remix | 9:16, hook in 1 s, captions burned in, 7–30 s |
| IG feed | Carousels (hook slide → 5–8 value slides → CTA slide), single static | 4:5; carousel slide 1 is the hook |
| X / Threads / LinkedIn | Hook line + short thread, founder lessons, build-in-public | Specific numbers, no hashtags walls |
| YouTube long-form | Tutorials, builds, breakdowns | Title + thumbnail are the hook (`thumbnails.md`) |
| Email | One idea, one link | Subject line = hook |

**Repurposing waterfall** — one core idea → 1 long piece (video/thread) → 3–5 short clips → 1 carousel → 3 single posts → 1 email. Plan the waterfall when you plan the idea.

**Content calendar** — deliver as a table: date · channel · pillar · format · hook · status. Default cadence unless the brain says otherwise: 1 short video/day per brand on the main channel, 2–3 carousels/week, 1 long piece/week.

**Captions** — line 1 is a second hook; 1–3 short lines of value; one CTA; 3–5 relevant hashtags max.

## 7. Compliance

- Health, skin, hair, money or results claims need substantiation — flag them, don't invent proof.
- Testimonials and reviews must be real. AI avatars speak as the brand or describe the product, never as a fake customer with personal results.
- Credit, employment, housing, social-issue offers → Meta Special Ad Category; flag it.
- Label realistic AI people where the platform requires it.
- Don't use other brands' logos or celebrity likenesses.
